Questions people ask first

Is this allowed on YouTube?

YouTube's policies apply to every channel, automated or not. Nightshift is designed around original, useful videos with direction set by you, and it supports YouTube's disclosure labels for altered or synthetic content. Every member gets a plain-language guide to the current rules.

Will my videos look like everyone else's?

Only if you want them to. You provide your voice, structure and examples of videos you like, and each channel gets its own style settings.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Setup is a short questionnaire, and connecting your YouTube account takes a couple of clicks.
